# ClipForge: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

ClipForge is a personal video-editing tool made and used by one person, Darian Danciu, for the GospelFuel channels that Darian owns (YouTube, the GospelFuel Facebook Page, the @gospelfuelclips Instagram account and the owner's TikTok accounts). It runs only on the owner's own Windows PC and is not offered to anyone else. There are no other users and no accounts; this page is its only public web presence.

## What ClipForge uses YouTube API Services for

- **Uploading** short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) that the owner edited in ClipForge to the owner's own channel (`youtube.upload` scope).
- **Reading the channel's name and picture** once at sign-in, so the app shows which channel is connected (`youtube.readonly` scope).

It does not read, collect or analyse anyone else's channels, videos, comments or personal data.

## What data is stored, and where

- The Google OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's channel, and the channel's ID, name and picture URL.
- They are stored in one file on the owner's own PC. They are never sent to any server other than Google's own APIs, and never shared, sold or used for advertising.
- The videos and their titles and descriptions are the owner's own content.

## Removing access and data

The owner can revoke ClipForge's access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>. Disconnecting the account in ClipForge deletes the stored tokens from the PC. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as YouTube's Developer Policies require.

## Third parties

ClipForge uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (<https://www.youtube.com/t/terms>), and Google's Privacy Policy applies (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>).

## Facebook and Instagram (Meta)

- ClipForge's Meta app ("ClipForge") is used only to **publish Reels** that the owner edited to the owner's own Facebook Page (GospelFuel) and Instagram account (@gospelfuelclips), and to read back the owner's own recent posts to confirm they went out.
- **Data stored:** the Page and Instagram access tokens, the Page ID and Instagram account ID, and their names and pictures. They are kept in one file on the owner's own PC, sent only to Meta's own APIs, and never shared, sold or used for advertising. No data about other people is collected.
- **Deleting your data:** disconnect the account in ClipForge (this deletes the stored tokens from the PC at once), or remove the "ClipForge" app at Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites (<https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications>). Anything ClipForge stored is gone once the tokens are deleted; to ask for deletion, email the contact below and it is done within 30 days.

## TikTok

- ClipForge's TikTok app ("GospelFuel ClipForge") is used only to **post videos** that the owner edited in ClipForge to the owner's own TikTok accounts, through TikTok's Content Posting API, and to show which account is connected.
- **Scopes:** `user.info.basic` (the connected account's display name and picture), `video.upload` and `video.publish` (sending the owner's finished video to the owner's own account, as a draft or a post).
- **Data stored:** the TikTok access and refresh tokens, the account's open ID, display name and picture. They are kept in one file on the owner's own PC, sent only to TikTok's own APIs, and never shared, sold or used for advertising. No data about other TikTok users is collected or read.
- **Deleting your data:** disconnect the account in ClipForge (this deletes the stored tokens from the PC at once), or remove the app in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions. To ask for deletion, email the contact below and it is done within 30 days.
- TikTok's Terms of Service (<https://www.tiktok.com/legal/terms-of-service>) and Privacy Policy (<https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy>) apply to the owner's use of TikTok.
